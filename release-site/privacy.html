Privacy

Draft for owner review. Updated 10 October 2026.

Cloud accounts and local-only use

For a cloud account, Daymark processes your email, authentication information, tasks, notes, deadlines, reminders and task history to provide your account and synchronize your work. Supabase provides cloud authentication and database storage. When authentication email delivery is enabled, the configured mail service processes recipients and account-action messages for authentication emails.

Local-only task workspaces stay on the device and do not synchronize their tasks to a cloud account. Daymark keeps device copies of cloud tasks and unsynced changes so you can work offline.

Device storage and reminders

Task databases use Android app-private storage without separate database encryption. Session credentials use Android Keystore protection. Reminders use Android notifications, subject to device permissions and operating-system restrictions. A reminder inbox entry records processing and does not guarantee notification delivery.

Daymark uses task history to show completion statistics. The current app includes no advertising or third-party tracking SDKs. Network services may process technical logs for security and operation.

Retention and deletion

Completing or deleting a task changes its state. Cloud task records, history and synchronization receipts remain associated with your account until account deletion. Clearing a device history cache does not erase cloud history. Local-only history can be cleared separately; active tasks and lifetime counts remain.

Account deletion removes your account and associated records from the live application database. Provider backups and operational logs follow provider and operator retention arrangements. Their retention periods are currently unknown and must be confirmed by the operator before this draft is published.

Offline copies on other devices are not remotely erased. Normal sign-out preserves device work. Clear Daymark storage on those devices to remove their copies; export work you want to keep first.

Exports

Device exports contain private tasks, notes, cached history and saved change records, including unreadable records retained for recovery. They exclude account credentials. Export files you save elsewhere remain under your control and are not removed by account deletion. An export is not a complete cloud backup.

You can delete a cloud account in Settings or through the account-deletion page.
