Privacy

Draft for owner review. Updated 13 November 2026.

What the app stores

Raheel Optics is an order book for an optical shop. For a cloud account it stores your email and sign-in information, and the shop records you enter: customer names and mobile numbers, eye prescriptions, frame and lens details, prices, payments, order dates, pickup reminders and notes. Supabase provides sign-in and database storage. Sign-up and password-reset codes are sent by email through the shop's configured mail account.

Guest mode keeps records only on the phone and does not upload them. Signed-in phones keep a copy of the shop's records and unsent changes so the app works offline.

Customer information

The shop is responsible for the customer information it records and for having a reason to keep it. Customer details are used only to manage orders, prepare invoices and send pickup or balance messages. WhatsApp messages are opened in WhatsApp for the shop to review and send; the app does not send them by itself.

Device storage and reminders

Records are kept in the app's private storage on the phone. Sign-in credentials are protected by the Android Keystore. Frame photos stay on the phone. Reminders use Android notifications and depend on the phone's notification settings.

The app contains no advertising or third-party tracking. Release builds send crash reports to Firebase Crashlytics so faults can be fixed; these contain technical details, not customer records. Network services may keep technical logs for security and operation.

Retention and deletion

Completed and cancelled orders stay in your account as shop records until you delete the account. Account deletion removes your account and its records from the live database. Provider backups and logs follow the provider's retention arrangements, which the operator must confirm before this page is published.

Copies on other phones are not erased remotely. Use Settings > Clear device data on those phones, after exporting anything you want to keep.

Exports

Exports contain your shop's orders and customers but not your password. Files you save elsewhere stay under your control and are not removed by account deletion.

You can delete your account in Settings or on the account-deletion page.

Contact

Developer identity and support contact must be configured before publication.
